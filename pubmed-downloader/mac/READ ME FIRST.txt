PubMed Downloader for Mac
=========================

1. Drag "PubMed Downloader" into your Applications folder (optional).

2. Double-click it. The first time, macOS warns that it can't check the
   app, because it isn't from the App Store. To allow it (only once):

   macOS 15 Sequoia or newer:
     - Click "Done" on the warning.
     - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
     - Scroll down to the message about PubMed Downloader, click
       "Open Anyway", and confirm.

   macOS 14 or older:
     - Right-click (or Control-click) the app, choose "Open", then
       click "Open" again.

3. If your Mac doesn't have Python yet, the app offers to install it.
   Click "Install", wait for it to finish, then open the app again.

4. The app opens in your web browser. Your papers are saved in the
   "PubMed Library" folder in your home folder.

To close the app, click "Quit" at the top right of its window. It also
closes by itself about 20 minutes after you close the browser tab
(unless a download is still running).

Something wrong? The app writes a log to:
  ~/Library/Logs/PubMed Downloader/app.log
